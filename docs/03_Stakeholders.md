# 3. Stakeholders

## 3.1 Overview

VakilConnect serves multiple stakeholders, each with distinct responsibilities and interactions within the platform. The system is designed to provide a seamless experience for clients seeking legal services, lawyers offering professional consultation, administrators managing the platform, and AI services assisting users.

---

## 3.2 Client

The client is the primary user of the platform who seeks legal consultation.

### Responsibilities

- Register and log in to the platform
- Search and filter lawyers
- View lawyer profiles
- Book appointments
- Upload legal documents
- Manage appointments
- Review and rate lawyers
- Interact with the AI Legal Assistant

---

## 3.3 Lawyer

Lawyers provide legal consultation services through the platform.

### Responsibilities

- Register as a lawyer
- Maintain professional profile
- Manage consultation availability
- Accept or reject appointment requests
- Access uploaded client documents
- Communicate with clients
- Manage appointment schedule

---

## 3.4 Administrator

The administrator manages the platform and ensures its smooth operation.

### Responsibilities

- Verify lawyer accounts
- Manage user accounts
- Monitor platform activity
- Handle reports and complaints
- Manage reviews
- View analytics
- Maintain platform integrity

---

## 3.5 AI Legal Assistant

The AI module assists users by providing intelligent legal guidance.

### Responsibilities

- Recommend suitable lawyers
- Explain legal terminology
- Summarize uploaded documents
- Answer basic legal questions
- Assist users during lawyer selection

---

## 3.6 External Services

VakilConnect integrates with external services to provide additional functionality.

### External Services

- Payment Gateway
- Cloud Storage
- Email Service
- SMS/WhatsApp Notification Service
- Large Language Model (LLM) API
