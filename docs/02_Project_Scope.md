# 2. Project Scope

## 2.1 Project Overview

VakilConnect is an AI-powered legal platform designed to simplify the process of discovering lawyers, booking legal consultations, and providing intelligent legal assistance. The system aims to connect clients with verified lawyers through a secure and user-friendly digital platform.

The project focuses on improving accessibility to legal services while providing lawyers with an efficient platform for managing appointments, client interactions, and professional profiles.

---

## 2.2 In Scope

The following features are included in Version 1 of VakilConnect:

### Client Module

- User Registration
- User Login
- Profile Management
- Search Lawyers
- Filter Lawyers
- View Lawyer Profiles
- Book Appointments
- Cancel Appointments
- View Appointment History
- Upload Legal Documents
- Rate & Review Lawyers

### Lawyer Module

- Lawyer Registration
- Profile Management
- Manage Availability
- Accept/Reject Appointments
- View Appointment Schedule
- View Client Details

### Admin Module

- Verify Lawyer Accounts
- Manage Users
- Manage Lawyers
- Manage Reviews
- View Platform Analytics

### AI Module

- AI Legal Assistant
- Lawyer Recommendation
- Legal Document Summarization
- Basic Legal Guidance

---

## 2.3 Out of Scope

The following features are not included in the first version:

- Online Court Filing
- Court Case Management
- Digital Signature Integration
- Video Consultation
- Online Arbitration
- Legal Notice Generation
- Multi-country Legal Support

These features may be considered in future releases.

---

## 2.4 Target Users

The primary users of VakilConnect are:

- Clients seeking legal consultation
- Verified Lawyers
- Platform Administrators

---

## 2.5 Expected Outcome

VakilConnect aims to provide a secure, scalable, and intelligent legal platform that reduces the effort required to find legal professionals, simplifies appointment management, and enhances the overall legal consultation experience through AI-powered assistance.
